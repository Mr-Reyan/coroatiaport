Bulgaria & The Black Sea Network

Bulgaria is a country in Southeast Europe, situated on the eastern side of the Balkan Peninsula. It borders Romania to the north, Serbia and North Macedonia to the west, Greece and Turkey to the south, and the Black Sea to the east. Its strategic location makes it a vital maritime hub connecting Europe with the Caucasus and the Middle East. Ship Chandler Bulgaria provides seamless vessel supply and marine procurement from our base in Varna, extending our rapid delivery network across all major Bulgarian ports.

Port of Varna

The largest port in Bulgaria and a major gateway for cargo, located on the Black Sea coast. It handles a massive volume of international trade.

Port of Burgas

The second-largest port in Bulgaria, located on the western coast of the Black Sea. It is a crucial hub for oil imports and petrochemicals.

Port of Balchik

A smaller port located on the northern Bulgarian coast, primarily serving local fishing fleets and offering picturesque views.

Port of Nessebar

A historic port town on the Black Sea coast, known for its ancient ruins and as a popular cruise ship destination.

Port of Sozopol

Another ancient port town, located south of Burgas. It serves as a marina for yachts and smaller vessels.

Port of Pomorie

A small port situated on a rocky peninsula, mainly used by fishing boats and offering shelter during storms.

Port of Tsarevo

A growing port and resort town in southeastern Bulgaria, serving both fishing vessels and recreational boats.

Port of Lom

A major river port on the Danube in northwestern Bulgaria, crucial for inland waterway transport.

Port of Ruse

The largest Bulgarian port on the Danube River, serving as a key link for trade with Romania and Central Europe.

Port of Vidin

A significant Danube port in northwestern Bulgaria, located near the border with Romania and Serbia.

Port of Silistra

A river port on the Danube, serving the northeastern region of Bulgaria and connecting to Romania.

Port of Svishtov

A historic Danube port town, known for its trade and as a crossing point between Bulgaria and Romania.
